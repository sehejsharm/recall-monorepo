## Full description (4000 char max)
```
Recall is the calm, no-nonsense way to prepare for competitive exams. Read a 2-minute micro-note, then drill rapid-fire multiple-choice questions on it. Every answer is graded instantly and scheduled with spaced repetition, so you review exactly when you're about to forget. No feeds, no fluff, no sign-up required.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
Your notes, questions, progress, XP and streaks live on your device. Study anywhere, signal or not. Make a free account only if you want to sync across devices and join the leaderboards.

BUILT FOR REAL EXAMS
• UPSC Civil Services — Polity, History, Geography, Economy, Current Affairs and more
• JEE Main & Advanced — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
• NEET UG — Biology, Chemistry, Physics
• SSC CGL — Quant, Reasoning, English, General Awareness
• GATE — Engineering Maths, Aptitude, Core CS
• CFA Program — Levels I, II and III
• FRM (GARP) — Parts I and II
Thousands of hand-crafted questions with clear explanations — curated by people, not AI slop.

WHY RECALL WORKS
• Active recall — retrieving the answer is the strongest way to remember it
• Spaced repetition (SM-2) — every card returns right before you'd forget it
• "Why each wrong answer is wrong" rationales — learn from the distractors
• A daily review that clears everything due across all your exams
• XP, combos, streaks and achievements that make daily practice stick
• An exam-date countdown so every session counts toward the day that matters
• Anonymized leaderboards — compete on XP while your real name stays private

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No ads. No trackers. We never sell your data. Your leaderboard identity is always anonymized, and you can delete your account and all cloud data from inside the app at any time.

Start with five quick cards and feel it working in two minutes.
```
