# Privacy Policy — Recall

_Last updated: 28 June 2026_

Recall ("the app") is an exam-preparation app for active-recall drilling and
micro-notes. We designed it to be private by default.

## What we collect

**Nothing is required.** The app is fully functional offline and anonymous.
Your study progress, spaced-repetition schedule, XP, streaks and bookmarks are
stored **only on your device** (SQLite). We cannot see them.

**If you choose to create an account** (optional), we use Supabase to store:

- your email address (for sign-in only);
- your study progress and read history (so it syncs across your devices);
- an **anonymized** leaderboard handle (e.g. "Aspirant-7QK4") plus your XP,
  level and streak counts. Your real name and email are **never** shown on the
  public leaderboard.

You can opt out of the leaderboard at any time in Settings, and you can delete
your account and all associated data on request (see Contact below).

## What we do not do

- We do **not** sell or share your data with third parties.
- We do **not** run ads or ad trackers.
- We do **not** collect location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for
  advertising.

## Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your
device. No notification data leaves your phone.

## Children

The app is intended for exam aspirants (generally 16+). We do not knowingly
collect personal data from children under 13.

## Data deletion

To delete your account and synced data, email the address below from your
account email and we will remove it within 30 days.
